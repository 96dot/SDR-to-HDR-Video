# Headroom HDR privacy policy

Headroom HDR does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- It has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. It makes no network requests of its own.
- Video frames are processed on your own graphics card and never leave your computer.
- Settings, per-site preferences and any model file you load are stored locally by your browser (`chrome.storage`) and are removed when you uninstall the extension.
- The diagnostic report is created only when you ask for it and is copied to your own clipboard. It contains technical details (graphics card, browser version, timings, settings) and the site's name, not the page address. It goes nowhere unless you paste it somewhere yourself.
- The optional "Unlock locked videos" switch changes response headers for video on a site you choose, in your own browser. It sends nothing anywhere.

Questions: open an issue on this project's GitHub page.
