Trained model

A model made with HDR Trainer can decide how bright each part of the picture gets, in place of the built-in shader. Everything else (colour, sharpening, roll-off to your display) works the same either way.

In HDR Trainer click Export model, then choose the file it wrote: hdr-trainer/workspace/export/hdr-model.json. The file stays on this computer.

Loaded model

Comparing it with the shader

In the popup, Method switches between the shader and the model.

Split shows two pictures at once on a video, one each side of a line you can drag. Its two menus pick what each side shows: the original, the shader or the model. So you can put the shader against the model, or the model against the untouched original.

With the model in use, the Reach, Shaping and Colour lights sliders do nothing, because they tune the shader's own brightness decisions. Peak brightness becomes a ceiling the model's highlights ease into.
